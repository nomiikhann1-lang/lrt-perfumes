# Editing the storefront

**Shared theme and header.** `app/theme.css` defines the plum, warm-white and lavender surface palette, shared buttons, focus states, search field and Perfumes dropdown. Family colours remain separate in `lib/store-config.js`. The three dropdown destinations and their matching rules are in `lib/collections.js`: a Collection value or product category containing the word Inspired selects Inspired Edition; other products select LRT Collection. Both landing pages remain available when empty. Small decorative heading labels and visible breadcrumb rows have been removed; product specifications and structured product data remain.

**Footer and social links.** Edit footer groups in `components/Footer.js` and compact mobile/desktop layouts in `components/Footer.module.css`. Phone sections expand individually. `components/SocialLinks.js` holds the Instagram, TikTok and Facebook destinations shared with Contact, plus the WhatsApp icon used on store pages.

**Storefront cards and store pages.** Store-shaped cards, daily opening hours and per-branch WhatsApp links are shared by Our Story, Our Stores and branch pages through `components/StoreVisits.js` and its CSS module. Edit each branch's existing address, phone, map and tint in `lib/story-content.js`. `lib/store-experiences.js` contains editable sample introductions and visit guidance for each location, plus shared opening hours. Review this editorial copy before a live release. Real store photography can replace the existing perfume campaign images through the store data.

**Bridge 2.6.0.** Upload the root `lrt-bridge.zip` in WordPress and replace the existing LRT bridge when prompted. Product editor and CSV no longer offer a custom bottle colour. The API, product save, sample importer and CSV importer derive it from the canonical scent family; the storefront also ignores old custom colour values. Existing photos, prices, stock and orders are preserved. The PHP palette in `wordpress/lrt-bridge/lrt-families.php` must match `familyColors` in `lib/store-config.js`; the test suite checks parity.

**Product scent backgrounds.** `components/ScentBackdrop.js` uses one faint family icon and two small ingredient accents along the outer edges, leaving the centre clear for the bottle. Ingredient art comes from the transparent 4-column × 3-row atlas `public/perfume-ingredients.png`; cells follow the ten-family order in `lib/store-config.js`. Generation details are in [product-ingredient-art.md](reports/product-ingredient-art.md). Product panels use a light family tint; the home slider keeps its own design. Styles are in `app/scent-backdrop.css`. Desktop cards reveal the decorations on hover or keyboard focus; touch screens and product detail images show them continuously. The layer is decorative, does not intercept taps, and respects reduced motion. It is shared by product grids, latest drops, related products, and product detail views.

**Sample catalogue.** The disconnected preview has 30 fictional products, with at least two examples in every scent family. The 20 additional examples are in `lib/demo-products.js` and carry a Demo badge. They use sample AED/QAR prices and 50/100/200 ml sizes. These entries are only used by demo mode; a connected WooCommerce catalogue supplies its own products.

**Home scent slider.** All ten families from `lib/store-config.js` appear on the home page, using their existing images in `public/scent-families/`. Edit slide background colours and captions in `lib/scent-slider.js`. Matching catalogue products use the shared bottle renderer, including uploaded WooCommerce photos; families without products show the family image and link to a filtered shop view. The product name opens its detail page. Layout and motion are in `components/ScentSlider.js` and `app/scent-slider.css`. Mobile tabs scroll horizontally and follow the active slide. Arrows, keyboard navigation within the hero, swipes, autoplay, pause, and reduced motion are supported. The hero pauses for six seconds after interaction and while a store dialog is open.

**Shop films.** In `lib/shop-promotions.js`, edit the title, button label, destination, MP4 and poster. Add media under public/ and use a path starting with /. Films rotate through the available campaigns. A new shop visit picks left or right edge positions after the first row. Positions stay stable while scrolling. Each film fills one mobile tile. Still pauses playback and Motion resumes it. Reduced-motion visitors start with the poster.

**Our Story and locations.** Edit addresses, phones, directions, Google Maps embed URLs, hero images and colours in `lib/story-content.js`. Edit the story layout in `components/OurStory.js` and the shared store template in `components/StoreVisits.js`. Store pages use /stores/ followed by the branch slug. Typography and interaction styles are in `app/storefront-v12.css`.

**Scent families.** Install the bundled bridge update in WordPress. In a product’s General tab, select one Scent family under LRT Perfumes scent details. The storefront uses it for both product-page labels and search icons. New CSV exports use the Scent family column. Older exports still import. The old labels Flowery, Earthy/Oud, Herbal, Warm and Gourmand map to Floral, Woody, Aromatic, Amber and Sweet respectively. A recognized Scent family takes priority over Legacy family on existing products. Products without a recognized family show no family icon.

Family colours are in `lib/store-config.js`. The ten small PNGs are in `public/scent-families/`.
